How to play

Phone: left thumb moves (push far to sprint). JUMP, HIT (jump then HIT for a flying kick), SNEAK to crouch, ACTION to talk and to take a guard down from behind.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, J to hit, E for action, C to sneak, Esc to pause.

Controller: left stick or d-pad to move, RT to sprint, A to jump, X to hit, Y for action, B to sneak, Start to pause.
