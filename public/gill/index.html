How to play

Phone: left thumb moves (push far to sprint). JUMP, HIT (jump then HIT for a flying kick), SNEAK to crouch, ACTION to talk and to take a guard down from behind.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, J to hit, E for action (hold to clear rubble), C to sneak, Q to boost once you've earned it, Esc to pause.

Controller: left stick or d-pad to move, RT to sprint, A to jump, X to hit, Y for action, B to sneak, LB to boost, Start to pause.

The beat: the dot by the clock pulses with the song. Hits on the beat do double damage, and the guards change shift every 8 bars.
