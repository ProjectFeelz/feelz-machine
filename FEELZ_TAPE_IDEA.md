# Feelz-Tapes (parked idea)

Status: parked for later. Noted 16 November 2026.

## The idea

A physical Feelz Machine tape player, plus physical cartridges people collect, called **Feelz-Tapes**.

- **Not artist albums on tape.** A Feelz-Tape is a Feelz Machine exclusive (Cynthia's idea): a fan builds their own tape like a playlist, choosing their favourite songs from Feelz Machine, then buys it.
- **We ship it out** with the device that plays it. The fan slots the tape into the player.
- **Collectable.** Feelz-Tapes are made to stack, display and collect.
- **Artist revenue.** At the right price, each Feelz-Tape sale gives a cut to the artists whose songs are on it.

## The player

- Built on a Raspberry Pi (better than an Arduino for audio playback).
- Physical buttons drive everything: play, skip, and moving through menus.
- Has a screen for showing menus, track info and artwork, but the screen is display only (no touch).

## Open questions for when we pick this up

- What a Feelz-Tape physically holds: real magnetic tape, or a tape shell with an NFC tag or SD card inside that the Pi reads.
- Licensing and rights for putting artists' songs on a physical product, and how the artist cut is set and paid (Feelz Machine takes no commission and must not hold artists' money, so the payout route needs to fit that).
- How many songs or minutes per tape, and pricing per Feelz-Tape and for the player.
- Tape builder in the app: a "build a Feelz-Tape" flow next to playlists, with cover art and a tape label.
- Manufacturing, enclosure design, shipping (starting in South Africa, then elsewhere).
- Whether a Feelz-Tape also unlocks the playlist in the Feelz Machine app.
